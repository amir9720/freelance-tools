How to Accurately Price Your Freelance Services

One of the biggest mistakes novice freelancers make is setting their hourly rate simply by dividing a previous salaried employee wage by 2,080 hours. In traditional employment, companies cover health insurance, hardware, office amenities, and paid leaves.

Understanding Non-Billable Time

In reality, you cannot bill 40 hours a week to clients. Tasks such as writing proposals, organizing your bookkeeping, replying to emails, and maintaining client portfolios are essential but unbillable. High-performing freelancers typically average between 60% and 75% billable efficiency.

The Pricing Rule:

Target Rate = (Net Target / (1 - Tax%) + Business Costs) / (Annual Billable Hours)
