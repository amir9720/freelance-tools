The Economics of Freelance Pricing

Navigating remote contracting requires a solid understanding of hidden fees and operational costs. Working independently offers flexibility, but failing to price in operational overhead or marketplace cuts will directly reduce your net take-home salary.

1. Factoring in Marketplace Deductions

Freelance marketplaces like Upwork and Fiverr provide access to high-paying international clients, but charge between 10% and 20% in transaction fees. When scoping a project on these networks, use our Take-Home Calculator to reverse-engineer your quotes so that your net income meets your lifestyle targets.

2. Why Billable Hours Are Not Total Hours

A standard full-time employee works 2,080 hours a year. As a freelancer, spending 40 hours a week on client deliverables is neither realistic nor sustainable. You must account for business development, client communications, bookkeeping, self-funded holidays, and potential periods between contracts. A healthy ratio is typically between 60% and 75% billable time.
