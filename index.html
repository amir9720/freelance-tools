Understanding Pet Energy Requirements (RER & DER)

Veterinary nutrition utilizes Resting Energy Requirement (RER) calculated as 70 × (Weight in kg)^0.75. From this baseline, Daily Energy Requirement (DER) multipliers account for growth, reproductive status, and activity levels. Feeding exact caloric allotments prevents feline hepatic lipidosis and canine obesity-related osteoarthritis.

Why Hydration Awareness Matters

Domestic felines have a biologically low thirst drive inherited from desert ancestors. When fed exclusively dry kibble containing less than 10% moisture, proactive hydration monitoring is crucial to deter feline lower urinary tract diseases (FLUTD).
